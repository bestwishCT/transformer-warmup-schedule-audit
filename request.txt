1: 5.2 Hardware and Schedule
2: We trained our models on one machine with 8 NVIDIA P100 GPUs. For our base models using
3: the hyperparameters described throughout the paper, each training step took about 0.4 seconds. We
4: trained the base models for a total of 100,000 steps or 12 hours. For our big models,(described on the

6: (3.5 days).
7: 5.3 Optimizer
8: We used the Adam optimizer [20] with β1 = 0.9, β2 = 0.98 and ϵ = 10 −9. We varied the learning
9: rate over the course of training, according to the formula:
10: lrate = d_model^(-0.5) * min(step_num^(-0.5), step_num * warmup_steps^(-1.5)) (3)
11: This corresponds to increasing the learning rate linearly for the first warmup_steps training steps,
12: and decreasing it thereafter proportionally to the inverse square root of the step number. We used
13: warmup_steps = 4000.
